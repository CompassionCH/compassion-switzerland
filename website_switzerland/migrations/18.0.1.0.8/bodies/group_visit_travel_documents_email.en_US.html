2. Travel documents

Please check that your passport will be valid six months after the date of your planned return to Switzerland. If this is not the case, please renew it in order to guarantee a worry-free trip.

Please check whether you need a visa to enter with your nationality and, if so, apply for it in time.

Compassion does not have comprehensive insurance for participants on sponsor trips. We recommend that you check your health and accident insurance coverage.
