Sign requests (OCA `sign_oca`) on employees, e.g. to follow who signed the
staff regulations:

- **Signed on paper**: on a draft or sent request, HR uploads the scan of the
  signed paper. The scan replaces the document and the signers who have not
  signed yet are marked as signed (activity log "Signed on paper", hash and
  inalterability sequence like an online signature). No e-mail asks them to
  sign.
- Once a request on an employee is fully signed (online or on paper), the
  signed PDF is posted in the chatter of the employee.
- "Signatures" button on the employee and "Employee" filter / group by on the
  sign requests.
