Sign requests (OCA ``sign_oca``) on employees, e.g. to follow who signed
the staff regulations:

-  **Signed on paper**: on a draft or sent request, HR uploads the scan
   of the signed paper. The scan replaces the document and the signers
   who have not signed yet are marked as signed (activity log "Signed on
   paper", hash and inalterability sequence like an online signature).
   No e-mail asks them to sign.
-  Once a request on an employee is fully signed (online or on paper),
   the signed PDF is posted in the chatter of the employee.
-  "Signatures" button on the employee and "Employee" filter / group by
   on the sign requests.

Usage
=====

1. Sign > Templates: template on the model "Employee", with a role whose
   partner is the expression ``{{object.work_contact_id.id}}``.
2. Employees list: select the employees, Actions > Sign from template.
3. An employee who signs on paper: open the request (Sign > Requests, or
   the "Signatures" button of the employee), "Signed on paper", upload
   the PDF scan.
4. Follow-up: Sign > Requests, group by state or employee.

"Signed on paper" needs the Sign group "User: All Documents".
