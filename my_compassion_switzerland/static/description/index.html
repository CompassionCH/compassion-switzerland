My Compassion Switzerland

Volunteer Engagement Portal

This module provides functionalities specific to the "My Compassion" portal for Compassion Switzerland, focusing on volunteer engagement.

Key Features

  • Volunteer Dashboard: Adds a new portal page at /my2/volunteering.
  • Rich Engagement Cards: Extends the advocate.engagement model to display opportunities with images, labels, and descriptions.
  • Prayer Subscription: Allows users to easily subscribe to or unsubscribe from the 'Prayer' engagement.
  • Volunteer Registration Form: A comprehensive form for users to register their interest.
  • Portal Security: Ensures only activated engagement types are visible on the portal.
