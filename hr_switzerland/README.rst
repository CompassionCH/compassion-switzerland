HR Management - Switzerland
===========================

Tailors HR department to Compassion CH needs.

Attendances
~~~~~~~~~~~

Compassion CH works on trust: employees can edit their own check-in and
check-out times. This module therefore:

* gives every internal user read, write, create and delete access to their
  own attendances, enforced by a record rule (other employees' attendances stay restricted to
  attendance officers and administrators);
* adds a *My Attendances* action, listing only the attendances of the current
  user;
* adds a *My Attendances* link in the check-in/check-out menu (the dot at the
  top of the screen) to open this list.
