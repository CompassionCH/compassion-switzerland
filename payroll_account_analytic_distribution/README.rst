This module splits the salaries of an employee among analytic accounts:
the employee contract gets an analytic distribution (for example ADM 30
% / Fundraising 70 %), given to the income and expense lines of the
journal entry of each payslip. The balance sheet lines get no analytic
distribution.

Without distribution on the contract, the analytic account of the
contract or of the salary rule is used, as in ``payroll_account``.

The distribution of a contract usually comes from a manual distribution
(an allocation key such as "HR-ADM", module
``account_analytic_distribution_manual``): a change of the key goes to
all its contracts (``account_analytic_distribution_manual_sync``).

On a database migrated from 14.0, the installation gives each contract
the manual distribution made of its former analytic tag.

When ``quality_test_compassion`` is installed, the installation also
creates the quality test "Payroll: payslips and their analytic split",
linked to the payroll modules: its responsible is notified to check the
payroll split after each update of these modules.

Usage
=====

On the employee contract, group *Accounting*: fill the *Analytic
Distribution* with the accounts and percentages of the employee. The
next payslips confirmed for this contract are split accordingly.
